# Quorra

**Quorra is a real browser that AI agents share, stay signed in to, and never fight over.** It runs on your own
server, keeps every account signed in, and gives every job its own private workspace. People can watch it all live
from any browser and take over in one click.

`v0.0.0` · runs on your own server · works with standard browser automation tools

---

## What you get

**▌ One browser, every account**
- **Signed in once, signed in for good.** Sign-ins survive restarts and updates.
- **Agents never handle your passwords.** The browser fills saved logins itself; agents never type, read or copy one.
- **One sign-in opens many doors.** Most sites ride on the browser's Google, Microsoft and Facebook sessions.
- **A daily sign-in check** flags any important account that has been signed out.

**▌ A private workspace for every job**
- **Every job gets its own named, colored workspace** and can only see and use its own pages.
- **Jobs can't step on each other,** and no job can close or break the shared browser.
- **Accounts are shared, work is separate.** Every job uses the same signed-in accounts; nobody's work gets mixed.

**▌ Humans and agents, side by side**
- **Live view.** Watch the browser from any modern browser at full resolution, and take control with one click.
- **No collisions.** You can type in one page while an agent fills a form in another. Tested: zero cross-typing.
- **Clean hand-offs.** When a site needs you (a sign-in, a code from your phone), the agent hands you that one page,
  you finish it, and it carries on.

**▌ The right browser for the job**
- **The main browser** for real, signed-in work.
- **Extra browsers on demand** for other identities, such as a client's own accounts.
- **Fast, lightweight sessions** with no sign-ins for public research, screenshots and PDFs.
- **Throwaway browsers:** spin up, use, gone.

**▌ Sign-ins you never lose**
- **Verified backups before every change.** Quorra proves the backup is good before it lets anything touch the
  browser, and refuses the change if it can't.
- **Nightly backups** on top, and a one-step restore.

**▌ Locked-down controls**
- Automation gets only the handful of controls it needs, and nothing more.
