# The PAF

**An AI team for running a small business that remembers, routes itself, and gets better every time it works.**

The Parallax Agent Framework turns Claude into a working team: not a chatbot, a staff. It learns your business,
builds itself around the work you actually do, and keeps getting sharper the longer it runs. Setup is a conversation,
not a project: the team interviews you, then gets to work.

`v1.1.8` · runs in the Claude desktop app and Claude Code · used daily to run real businesses

---

## What you get

**▌ A full team from day one**
- **Seventeen specialist advisors** covering strategy, finance, legal, tax, risk, sales, marketing, research, data,
  project management, AI strategy and a three-part security team.
- **A team built around your business.** After onboarding, the framework builds the delivery roles your work
  actually needs, in the same shape and standards as the rest of the team.
- **You never pick the expert.** Ask in plain words; the right specialists take it and come back with one answer.
  They consult each other without being asked.

**▌ It runs itself**
- **Built lean.** Designed from the ground up to keep running costs low, so the team can work all day without
  burning through its budget.
- **Learns new tools on its own.** Connect a new tool or show it a new idea, and the team works out who should use it
  and starts using it, without being reminded.
- **Never asks you twice.** If you ever have to repeat a request, the team treats that as its own bug and fixes it.

**▌ It gets better every week**
- **Every agent grades its own work.** Advisors compare what they recommended with what actually happened and spot
  their own patterns.
- **A weekly team review** finds repeated manual work, missing skills and recurring mistakes, fixes the small ones
  on the spot and brings the rest to you for a yes or no.
- **It absorbs what it meets.** Show it a tool, a technique or another team's approach and it adopts the useful part,
  rebuilt in its own way.

**▌ It remembers, and keeps its memory true**
- **Nothing is lost in chat.** What the team learns is written down the same day and used on the next job.
- **Never a stale answer.** Prices, plans and decisions are kept current, and the team checks them before it uses them.

**▌ It stays true to your business**
- **Your facts, your voice.** The team speaks the way your business speaks and quotes your real, current prices.
- **Works with the tools you have.** Swap a tool and the team carries on.
- **Bookkeeping from day one,** with your accountant's rules winning every time.

**▌ It works with people, and with other teams**
- **Multiple people and departments,** with nobody duplicating anyone's work.
- **Alliances.** Two businesses running the PAF can work on a joint project together while each keeps its own team
  and its own data sealed.

**▌ Safe by design**
- **Human in the loop.** It never sends a customer message, publishes, signs, moves money or makes an irreversible
  change without your go-ahead.
- **Zero fabrication.** "I don't know, here is how I'll find out" is the expected answer.
