# The Grid changelog

Newest first. Plain English.

## Unreleased
- **IO Tower: Net Watch.** Site Watch grew into Net Watch: tile cards for sites, servers and the programs inside them, 24-hour line graphs, findings that open to a plain-English report with a Fix it button, a fix log, and a live view of any server redrawn every two seconds. Light screenshots, sample data only.
- **Knowledge Graph: flight paths and a timeline.** Tick any routines and each flies its own color on curved paths; a timeline plays back the share's day, week or whole history, with routines lighting up while they run and files popping, pulsing, gliding and fading. Light screenshot, sample data only.
- **IO Tower: My Library.** Every person gets a private library tab of their own; a card goes public only when its owner says so. Light screenshot, sample data only.
- **IO Tower: Learning Library media and chips.** Podcasts play at the top of an open card, videos and infographics under its own cards, and sources, links and files show as chips by kind. Light screenshot, sample data only.
- **IO Tower: Learning Library drill-down.** An open card shows its related cards on a small shelf, tap to go deeper, Back to come up. Light screenshot, sample data only.
- **IO Tower: the team's agenda on your calendar.** What the team is watching for and the routines it will run sit on your calendar, with one tick box to hide them and a Team Agenda list. Light screenshot, sample data only.
- **IO Tower: Learning Library.** Every research card on one shelf of cards (learned, learning now, up next), each opening big to read in full, with search over every word. New Light screenshot, sample data only.

- **Uplink: Read to me, and a private voice.** Share an article, PDF or Word file to Uplink and it reads it aloud, with Pause and Stop. Answers are spoken in a natural voice made on your own server. New Light screenshot on a sample phone.

- **IO Tower: the team talks back in a private voice.** The voice page speaks answers, and the Listen button on any message reads it, in a natural voice made on your own server.

- **IO Tower: Search Watch.** A new section on the IO Tower page: how people find a business on Google, on the map and in AI answers, month by month, with a tile per business and a full page. New screenshots (the board, a page in Light and Dark, the map grid), sample data only.

- **DataDisk: Read it to me.** Any note, PDF, Word file or web page can be read aloud in a natural voice made on your own server, so the words stay in the house. New Light screenshot, sample data only.

- **IO Tower: the team's work shows on your calendar.** A calendar item whose card the team is working on shows what it is doing right now, with a timer, under its title. New Light screenshot, sample data only.

- **Uplink: Auto Except and the big screen.** One switch stops the Yes prompts for everything but money, and the team can now work on a monitor plugged into the phone. New Light screenshot of the two switches on a sample phone.
